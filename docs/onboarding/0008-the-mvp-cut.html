6 · Strength & weakness (mission lens)

Strength

The cut is disciplined and documented — every subtraction is an ADR with a stated reversal and a "revisit later" hook, not silent scope-creep-in-reverse. Removing Postgres, the org rollup, and the stateful features makes the MVP genuinely shippable and small. The architecture stays additive: deferred pieces slot back in without a rewrite.

Weakness / risk

The gap between documented system and shipped system is wide (L06) — and the ADR headers are the only place the reversals are recorded; the prose docs (CLAUDE.md) still describe the pre-cut world. Anyone onboarding from CLAUDE.md inherits a false model. The single-scope budget also means no org-level ceiling — total spend is visible in ADX but not enforced.
