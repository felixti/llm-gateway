1 · The three strengths (defend these)

Three things this system gets genuinely right

  1. Clean separation of concerns, plane by plane and hop by hop. Two planes split on "generic vs LLM-domain" with first-party Entra validation at the edge (L01); a fail-closed pipeline where each hop has one job and talks only through typed context vars (L02); vertical-slice modules that test in isolation (L07).
  2. Correct-by-construction money & metering. Atomic Lua budget reserve/commit that's last-dollar-safe and cluster-native via the hash-tag (L04); metering that's decoupled, layered, self-healing, and effectively-once through the ADX dedup view (L05).
  3. Defense-in-depth without operational weight. A soft internal trust boundary held by four independent layers — no mTLS burden, yet a forged header is useless (L03); and an honest, tiny live proxy that fails closed on what it can't do (L06/L07).

2 · The three weaknesses (name these first)

Three things to flag before anyone else does

  1. The docs describe a system you don't run. CLAUDE.md documents Bun, Postgres, PATs, circuit breaker, retry, streaming — all legacy or deferred. This is the most dangerous gap because it hides the others (L06/L08).
  2. No upstream resilience, and money leaks on failure. No breaker, no retry, faked streaming (L06); the budget charges the 1.2× estimate not actuals and doesn't release a reservation on upstream failure (L04).
  3. Soft edges that are fine now, risky later. Trust layer ④ (JWS) unbuilt → a valid-M2M insider can spoof claims (L03); ADX ingest is stubbed and WAL defaults to volatile /tmp (L05); single budget scope = no enforced org ceiling (L08); the SP-project-via-roles M0 hack (L03).
